Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 11, 2026

MAYISH Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") operates the VlogRecorder application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, you may provide your email address and other registration information.
  • Subscription Information: Information about your subscription status and purchase history, processed through RevenueCat.
  • Communications: Any information you provide when contacting us via email.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the App, including recording settings, features accessed, and session duration.
  • Camera Settings: Your preferences for video quality, frame rate, and aspect ratio.

1.3 Video Content

Video content you record using VlogRecorder is stored locally on your device and saved directly to your photo library. We do not upload, transmit, or store your recorded videos on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To manage your subscription and process purchases
  • To save videos to your photo library upon your request
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To detect and prevent technical issues
  • To improve and optimize the App experience
  • To communicate with you regarding App updates or support

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat processes your payment information and subscription data according to their privacy policy. We receive only subscription status information from RevenueCat, not your payment details.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

3.2 Apple App Store

All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. Your transaction history and payment information are governed by Apple's privacy policy and terms of service.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults.
  • Keychain: Sensitive data such as free trial status is stored securely in your device's Keychain.
  • Photo Library: Recorded videos are saved directly to your device's photo library, subject to your device's privacy settings.

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: Required to record video content.
  • Microphone: Required to capture audio with your videos.
  • Photo Library: Required to save your recorded videos to your photo library and access videos for editing.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to delete your data
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

If you are accessing the App from outside the jurisdiction in which we are established, please note that your information may be transferred across borders. We will take appropriate measures to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Subscription and purchase records may be retained for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

11. Cookies and Tracking

VlogRecorder does not use cookies or tracking technologies to collect personal information. We may use anonymous analytics tools to understand App usage patterns, which do not identify individual users.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Effective Date". Your continued use of the App after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
