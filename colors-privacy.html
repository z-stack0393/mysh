Privacy Policy

Colorize Photos · Effective and last updated August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Colorize Photos (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, how it is collected and used, who receives it, and the choices available to you.

AI processing disclosure: Colorize Photos sends a resized copy of the photo you select, your selected color style, and — when you use Pro mode — your selected output resolution to a third-party AI processing provider to generate the image you request. In Flash mode the photo is processed by Alibaba Cloud Model Studio; in Pro mode the photo is processed by RapidAPI (the Palette photo colorization service). The App asks for your explicit permission before the first upload or AI request. For example, an old black-and-white family photo is sent so it can be colorized and returned to you.

2. AI Processing and Your Permission

Before the App uploads a photo for AI processing, it presents an in-app notice identifying:

  • what will be sent: the selected photo, the selected color style, and — for Pro mode — the selected output resolution;
  • who receives it: Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Flash mode) or RapidAPI, the Palette photo colorization service (Pro mode); and
  • why it is sent: to produce the restoration, colorization, or edit you requested.

Nothing is uploaded for this purpose unless you tap Agree & Continue. If you choose Not Now, the photo remains on your device and no AI request is made. Your permission is stored on your device.

You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Privacy → AI Data Sharing. Withdrawal prevents future uploads and AI sharing unless you agree again. It does not automatically delete images already in your cloud History; those can be deleted individually, or you can delete your account and all associated media from Settings.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Cloud History: selected original photos, generated results, and associated job records are retained while they remain in your History so you can access them again.
  • Individual deletion: deleting an item from History deletes its job record and associated media when they are no longer referenced by another job.
  • Account deletion: using Delete Account in Settings deletes your account, History, and stored media from our active systems.
  • Transactions and security records: limited purchase, fraud-prevention, security, legal, and backup records may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain system integrity.
  • Provider processing: service providers may keep limited request or security records according to their applicable retention rules and our service arrangements. We require them not to retain personal information longer than necessary to provide the service or meet legal obligations.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may:

  • withdraw future AI data-sharing permission in the App’s Settings;
  • delete individual images and jobs from History;
  • delete your account and stored media in Settings;
  • manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings; or
  • contact us to make a privacy request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children’s Privacy
